# Ratoon Rice — Green Food Safety, Low-Carbon Yield-Increasing Technologies, and Industrial Policies

**再生稻绿色食品安全与低碳增产技术及产业政策研究**

Research on the green transformation of the ratooning rice sector in Fujian, China, from "selling raw grain" to "selling green products" and from a "subsidy-driven" to a "commercial-driven" model.

## Overview

This project addresses the synergy dilemma of safety, low-carbon emission, and income generation in Fujian's ratooning rice sector. It targets two bottlenecks: balancing heavy-metal risk management with carbon-sink potential, and aligning policy with market mechanisms.

The core pathway integrates four components — regional suitability assessment, insurance compensation, carbon-sink accounting, and brand premium — to monetize green value while sustaining farmer income growth. The goal is a reproducible "Fujian Model" that supports the national target of 10 million mu (≈ 667,000 ha) of ratooning rice.
